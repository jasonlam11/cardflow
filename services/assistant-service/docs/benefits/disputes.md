# Disputing a Charge

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## When to dispute a charge
You can dispute a charge that you don't recognize, were charged twice for, never received, or that was not as described. Try contacting the merchant first for non-fraud issues.

## Dispute deadline
Disputes must be filed within 60 days of the statement date on which the charge first appeared.

## What happens after you dispute
We issue a provisional credit for the disputed amount within 10 business days while we investigate. Investigations take up to 90 days. If the charge is found valid, the provisional credit is reversed and we explain why.

## Fraud versus merchant disputes
Charges you did not make are handled as fraud and covered by zero liability. Problems with a purchase you did make are merchant disputes, and you may need to provide receipts, correspondence or proof of return.
