# Purchase Protection

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## What is covered
Eligible items bought entirely with your CardFlow Card are covered against accidental damage and theft for 120 days from the purchase date.

## Coverage limits
Purchase protection pays up to $1,000 per claim and up to $50,000 per account per calendar year. Payment is the lesser of the repair cost or the purchase price.

## What is not covered
Motor vehicles and their parts, consumable or perishable items, items that are lost or simply disappear, items left unattended in a public place, used or pre-owned items, and normal wear and tear are not covered.

## How to file a purchase protection claim
File within 90 days of the damage or theft. Include the receipt, your card statement, and a police report for theft.
