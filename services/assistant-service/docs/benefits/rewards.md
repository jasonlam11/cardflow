# Rewards

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## Earning rates
- 4 points per dollar at restaurants, cafes and food delivery
- 3 points per dollar on travel: airlines, hotels and rideshare
- 2 points per dollar at grocery stores, on up to $6,000 of grocery spend per calendar year, then 1 point per dollar
- 1 point per dollar on all other eligible purchases

## Purchases that don't earn points
Money transfers, gambling and betting, gift card purchases, balance transfers, cash advances, fees and interest do not earn points.

## Redeeming points
Each point is worth 1 cent as a statement credit, so 10,000 points are worth $100. There is no minimum to redeem.

## Do points expire
Points do not expire as long as your account is open and in good standing. If the account is closed, unredeemed points are forfeited.
