# Travel Insurance

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## Trip cancellation and interruption
If you pay for a trip with your CardFlow Card, we reimburse prepaid, non-refundable travel costs when the trip is cancelled or cut short for a covered reason. Coverage is up to $5,000 per trip and $10,000 per 12-month period, per cardholder.

Covered reasons include illness or injury of you or a travel companion, severe weather that stops travel, jury duty, and the death of an immediate family member.

## What trip cancellation does not cover
Changing your mind, pre-existing medical conditions, travel booked with points from another program, and cancellations caused by your employer are not covered.

## Trip delay
If a common carrier (airline, train, bus or cruise) delays your trip by more than 6 hours, or requires an overnight stay, we reimburse reasonable meals, lodging and essential items up to $500 per ticket. At least part of the fare must be paid with your CardFlow Card.

## Baggage delay
If your checked bags are delayed by more than 12 hours, we reimburse essential purchases such as clothing and toiletries up to $100 per day for up to 3 days.

## Lost luggage
If the carrier permanently loses your checked or carry-on bags, we cover the difference between the carrier's payment and the value of the bag and its contents, up to $3,000 per traveler.

## How to file a travel claim
File within 60 days of the incident. Include your card statement showing the purchase, the itinerary, and proof of the delay or loss from the carrier.
