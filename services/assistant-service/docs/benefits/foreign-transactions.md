# Using Your Card Abroad

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## Foreign transaction fees
The CardFlow Card has no foreign transaction fees. You pay no extra percentage on purchases made in another currency or processed outside the US.

## Exchange rates
Purchases in another currency are converted to US dollars at the exchange rate set by the card network on the day the transaction is processed.

## Paying in local currency
If a merchant offers to charge you in US dollars instead of the local currency (dynamic currency conversion), choose the local currency. The merchant's conversion rate is usually worse than the network rate.

## Traveling with your card
You do not need to set a travel notice. Fraud monitoring takes your purchase history into account, and we may contact you to confirm unusual transactions.
