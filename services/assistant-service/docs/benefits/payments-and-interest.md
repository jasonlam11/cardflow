# Payments and Interest

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## Statements and due dates
You get a monthly statement. Your payment is due 25 days after the statement closing date.

## Avoiding interest
Pay the full statement balance by the due date every month and you will not be charged interest on purchases. This 25-day period is the grace period.

## Interest rate
The purchase APR is 21.99%, variable. Interest is charged on balances you carry past the due date.

## Minimum payment
The minimum payment is the greater of $35 or 1% of the balance plus interest and fees. If your balance is under $35, the minimum is the full balance.

## Late payments
A late payment fee of up to $40 applies if you don't make at least the minimum payment by the due date.

## Autopay
You can set up autopay for the minimum, the statement balance, or a fixed amount.
