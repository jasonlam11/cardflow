# Extended Warranty

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## How the extended warranty works
When you buy an item with your CardFlow Card, we extend an eligible US manufacturer's warranty by 1 additional year. The original warranty must be 3 years or less. No registration is needed.

## Extended warranty limits
Coverage is up to $10,000 per item and $50,000 per account. Repairs are covered up to the original purchase price.

## What the extended warranty excludes
Items with no original manufacturer's warranty, cosmetic damage, software, and items bought for resale are excluded.

## How to file a warranty claim
File within 60 days of the item failing. You will need the receipt, the original warranty, and a repair estimate.
