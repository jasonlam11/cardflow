# Lost or Stolen Card

> Fictional benefits for the CardFlow demo project. Not a real financial product or offer.

## Zero liability
You are not responsible for unauthorized charges made with your lost or stolen card, as long as you report them promptly.

## What to do right away
Freeze the card immediately, then report it lost or stolen by calling the number on the back of your card or contacting support. Freezing stops new purchases but does not stop recurring payments you already set up.

## Replacement cards
A replacement card with a new number arrives within 2 business days. Expedited shipping is free.

## After you report it
Review your recent transactions and tell us about any you don't recognize. We will open a fraud claim for each one and remove confirmed fraudulent charges.
